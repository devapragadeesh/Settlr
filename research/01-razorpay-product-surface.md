# Razorpay Product Surface & Strategic White Space

## Core acceptance (mature, GA)
Payment Gateway, Payment Links, Payment Pages, Payment Buttons, QR Codes, Invoices, Subscriptions, Offers, Webstore, EMI. Smart Collect 2.0 provides virtual accounts for NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/UPI. Route handles split settlements to linked accounts.

## Growth bets
Magic Checkout (one-click checkout). Optimizer (multi-PA/multi-gateway routing plus Single View Reconciliation across payment gateways). Razorpay Trusted Business and Buyer Protection form a trust layer.

## Banking and adjacent
RazorpayX (current account, payouts, payout links). Razorpay Capital (instant settlements, business loans). XPayroll. POS/Ezetap. Thirdwatch (RTO and fraud, merged into Magic Checkout). Curlec (Malaysia). Singapore entry 2026.

## Newest and most strategically loaded
Razorpay Vulcan is a transformer-based payments foundation model built with NVIDIA and AWS, doing real-time routing, network-level fraud detection, COD risk scoring. UNVERIFIED beyond press release.

Razorpay Agent Studio is built on Anthropic's Claude Agent SDK. VERIFIED at razorpay.com/agent-studio. Ships seven agents, all early access: Dispute Responder, Subscription Recovery, Abandoned Cart Conversion, RTO Shield, RTO Insights, Settlement Insights, Cashflow Forecaster.

Agentic Payments runs on NPCI Single Block Multi Debit mandates and UPI Reserve Pay. Pilots with Zomato, Swiggy, Zepto.

## Strategy
IPO: confidential DRHP filed with SEBI June 2026. The margin problem drives everything — UPI is zero-MDR, so take rate compresses as UPI mix grows. The AI pivot is the answer to margin-thin payments. Leadership positioning is moving from payments provider to financial operating system for businesses.

## White space, ranked
1. Agentic-commerce merchant-side infrastructure. Razorpay has consumer rails but no merchant-facing product for being sold to by an agent: agent-readable catalog, agent identity and trust scoring, per-agent spend policy.
2. Mandate and subscription lifecycle intelligence.
3. Reconciliation as a product, not a report. Merchants get net-only reporting with no transaction linkage, missing UTRs, untraceable deductions, and GST on MDR needing a separate line for input tax credit.
4. Cashflow and working-capital control plane.
5. Dispute evidence supply chain.
